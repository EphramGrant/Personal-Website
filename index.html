Favorite Artists

Radiohead

Alt Rock · Active since 1993

My favorite songs are How to Disappear Completely, Let Down, and Man of War. Discography ranked:

  1. 1 OK Computer (Near Perfect Album)
  2. 2 In Rainbows (Amazing but a few songs are just ok)
  3. 3 Kid A (High highs but I dislike a few songs such as the title track)
  4. 4 A Moon Shaped Pool (Extremely heartfelt and very consistent album)
  5. 5 Hail to the Thief (Underrated with maybe their best open/closing tracks)
  6. 6 The Bends (A little overrated but still amazing)
  7. 7 Amnesiac (Some good songs and life in a glasshouse is awesome)
  8. 8 The King of Limbs (Very laidback album but codex is a top 10 radiohead song)
  9. 9 Pablo Honey (Creep is radioheads best song)
